# Credibility review

Read the actual transcript or caption and inspect analysis for available visual proof.
Distinguish a demonstrated result, creator assertion, and missing evidence. Do not
claim external fact-checking: no external research tool is available here.
Compare the promise with this persona's needs, expertise, and skepticism. Identify
the most relevant unresolved objection, without inventing price or features.
Recommend a concrete way to show proof. Explain what the viewer understood and
what remains uncertain. Keep trust, relevance, and conversion intent separate;
credibility alone does not imply purchase intent.
