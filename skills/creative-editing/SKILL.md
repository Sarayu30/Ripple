# Creative editing

Read evidence_summary and panel_feedback. Select the highest-value confusion or
objection from actual viewer feedback. Propose a concrete change to opening,
demonstration, or CTA and explain which evidence supports it. Keep the original
message recognizable. Return at most three candidate edits and two distinct A/B
variants. Never promise better engagement or invent an observed frame or timestamp.
