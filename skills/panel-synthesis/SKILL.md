# Panel synthesis

Use write_todos to plan a short evidence review, creative review, and synthesis.
Use panel_feedback to inspect completed viewers and evidence_summary for content
limitations. Delegate one evidence-review task and one creative-editor task; keep
their findings separate until both return. Pass task-specific data or ask them
to use their read-only tools. No specialist may create or revise persona scores.
Use disagreeing or skeptical viewers as well as positive feedback. Cite viewer
IDs in edit rationales when evidence supports them. Prioritize exactly three edits,
an alternative hook, caption, CTA, cover, A/B variants, and what to keep. Do not
invent observed video details, external research, statistical confidence, or uplift.
Finish with a concise evidence-grounded recommendation brief for schema validation.
